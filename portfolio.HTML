About

Turning data into models, and models into products

I'm Kannadhasan, a final-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. I like problems where messy data has to become something a person can actually use.

My work combines Python-based machine learning with full-stack JavaScript, so I can train and evaluate a model, then build the Node.js backend and web interface around it. I enjoy the whole path, from cleaning a dataset in Pandas to shipping the screen someone clicks on.

I'm looking for a team where I can keep learning from experienced engineers while contributing to real data and AI work.
